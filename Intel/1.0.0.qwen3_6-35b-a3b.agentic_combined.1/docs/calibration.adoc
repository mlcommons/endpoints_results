= Calibration and Model Transformations

== Source model and weight transformation

The INT8 checkpoint was produced from the BF16
`Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B` model at revision
`995ad96eacd98c81ed38be0c5b274b04031597b0`.

The recipe uses `llmcompressor==0.13.0` for data-free INT8 W8A8
round-to-nearest (RTN) quantization of language-model projections, including
MoE expert `gate_proj`, `up_proj`, and `down_proj` weights.
Weights use per-channel quantization; activations use dynamic tokenwise
quantization. The corpus below is used only to calibrate FP8 KV-cache scales.

Token embeddings, the output head, and routing gates (`mlp.gate` and
`mlp.shared_expert_gate`) are excluded. Source BF16 normalization weights
are restored after compression. Multimodal components are outside the recipe
scope, and MTP was disabled in the submitted runs.

== FP8 KV-cache calibration

Static, symmetric, tensorwise 8-bit FP8 KV-cache scales are calibrated with
the `static_minmax` observer. `llmcompressor.oneshot` uses ten samples,
batch size 1, no sample shuffling, and a maximum sequence length of
16,384 tokens.

The source is the Agentic Inference performance dataset
(`mlperf_agentic_inference_dataset.jsonl`), extracted from the benchmark
client image and verified against SHA-256
`1beb24c882122df96571cf11b390acbea388944038bc55c78b891475459014ae`.

Using the tokenizer at the model revision above, the generator renders chat
prefixes and selects ten distinct conversations with seed 42. It prefers
prefixes of 10,240–16,384 tokens; longer prefixes fill any remaining places
and are truncated to their final 16,384 tokens.

Forward passes over these tokenized prefixes provide the KV observations.
Prefixes may include earlier assistant and tool messages; their content is
used for calibration. No reference-completion labels or gradient-based
training are used.
